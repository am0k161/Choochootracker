Mix and match as much as you want!

- **Braids, Plaits & Plaits-Alt** — explore digital oscillators, physical models, textures and percussion from the Mutable Instruments family and its extensions.
- **Classic chips** — AY, SID, Game Boy and Sega voices, alongside OPL, OPLL, VRC7, Genesis, arcade and DX7 FM synthesis.
- **Achchid** — an acid voice based on Open303, with the option of a Braids oscillator.
- **Bogie & Sintered** — drum synthesis, from familiar hits to unruly percussion.
- **MME** — an experimental voice built around interacting modulation and waveshaping.
- **MonkSynth** — a vocal synthesiser with vowels, breath, vibrato and layered voices.
- **Samples & wavetables** — play your recordings, blend single-cycle waveforms or bring your own Serum wavetables.
- **SoundFonts** — bring sampled instrument collections into your tracks.
