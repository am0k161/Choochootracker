The fast, LSDJ-inspired workflow from ChipNomad, with ideas borrowed from Elektron and NerdSEQ. Write notes into phrases, link them into chains and build a song across eight independent tracks.

- **Make every step count.** Change a sound, add a slide or set a chance of playing, right beside the note.
- **Let patterns evolve.** Play tracks at different speeds, add groove and use conditions to vary each pass.
- **Play your changes.** Move the joysticks to shape a sound and record that movement into your sequence.

Three effect columns per step, reusable phrases, chains and modulation tables give a small pattern plenty of room to grow.
